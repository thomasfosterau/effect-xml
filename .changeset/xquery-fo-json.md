`XQuery.evaluate` implements the JSON functions of Functions and Operators 3.1: `parse-json` and `json-doc` (with the `duplicates`, `escape` and `fallback` options; `liberal` accepts only standard JSON), `json-to-xml` (`validate` is not supported and fails with `FOJS0004`) and `xml-to-json`.
