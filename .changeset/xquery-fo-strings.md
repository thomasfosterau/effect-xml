`XQuery.evaluate` implements the string functions of Functions and Operators 3.1: `codepoints-to-string`, `string-to-codepoints`, `compare`, `codepoint-equal`, `substring`, `normalize-space`, `normalize-unicode`, `upper-case`, `lower-case`, `translate`, `contains`, `starts-with`, `ends-with`, `substring-before`, `substring-after`, `contains-token`, `collation-key`, `default-collation`, `encode-for-uri`, `iri-to-uri` and `escape-html-uri`, with the codepoint and HTML ASCII case-insensitive collations (other collations fail with `FOCH0002`); and the regular-expression functions `matches`, `replace`, `tokenize` and `analyze-string`, with XPath's regular expressions (anchors, capturing and non-capturing groups, back-references, reluctant quantifiers) and the `s`, `m`, `i`, `x` and `q` flags.
