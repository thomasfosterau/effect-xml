`XQuery.evaluate` implements the numeric functions of Functions and Operators 3.1 (`abs`, `ceiling`, `floor`, `round` and `round-half-to-even` with a precision, each keeping the input's primitive numeric type), the aggregates `sum`, `avg`, `min` and `max` (numeric promotion, durations, `NaN`, collations for strings), the `math:` functions (`pi`, `exp`, `exp10`, `log`, `log10`, `pow`, `sqrt`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `atan2`), and `random-number-generator`, a deterministic generator per seed with `number`, `next` and `permute`.
